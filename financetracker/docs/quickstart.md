## Getting Started in 5 Minutes

### Step 1: Add Your First Account

1. Go to **Manage → Accounts** (or click toolbar)
2. Click **Add Account**
3. Enter account name (e.g., "Main Bank")
4. Select type: Checking / Savings / Credit Card
5. Set initial balance
6. Click **Add Account**

### Step 2: Add Categories

1. Go to **Manage → Categories**
2. Create expense categories:
   - 🏠 Housing
   - 🍔 Food & Dining
   - 🚗 Transportation
   - 🛒 Shopping
   - 💊 Healthcare
3. Create income categories:
   - 💰 Salary
   - 💵 Freelance
   - 🎁 Gifts

### Step 3: Add Your First Transaction

1. Click **Add Transaction** button (toolbar)
2. Fill in details:
   - **Date**: Select date
   - **Amount**: Enter amount
   - **Description**: What was it for?
   - **Type**: Income or Expense
   - **Category**: Select category
   - **Account**: Select account
3. Click **Save**

### Step 4: View Monthly Summary

- Use **<** and **>** buttons to navigate months
- Left panel shows:
  - Total Income
  - Total Expenses
  - Current Balance
